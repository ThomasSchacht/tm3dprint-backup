Privacy Policy

TM3DPRINT Backup

Last updated: November 1, 2026

1. Purpose

TM3DPRINT Backup is an internal backup application operated by TM3DPRINT I/S. It uses Google Drive to store encrypted backups of our website, databases, and server files.

2. Google Drive Access

The application uses Google OAuth to obtain authorized access to Google Drive for backup storage and restoration.

Google Drive access is used only for managing the organization's backup data.

3. Data Protection

Backup data is encrypted using Restic before being uploaded to Google Drive. Encryption credentials are managed separately from the stored backup data.

4. Data Sharing

TM3DPRINT Backup is not a public customer application. Google Drive is used as an external storage provider for encrypted backups. Google user data is not sold or used for advertising.

5. Data Retention

Backups are retained according to the organization's backup and retention procedures. Obsolete backups may be deleted as part of routine maintenance.

6. Access Revocation

Google account access can be revoked through the Google Account security settings. Revoking access prevents further authorized backup operations until access is restored.
